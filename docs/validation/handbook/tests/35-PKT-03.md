## Steps

1. Level 0. Note the estimate and the chip-load warning (expected: adaptive's thin chip at the fixture's feed; the warning suggests a feed).
2. Level 1. Watch the helical entry and the loops: smooth, with no stutter between short moves. Time it.
3. Level 3. Listen: the load should sound even, without the heavier cut of an offset pocket's corners. Time it again.
4. Optional: in the app, switch the pocket's strategy to Offset, cut a second pocket in the same wood, and compare the sound, chips and time.
