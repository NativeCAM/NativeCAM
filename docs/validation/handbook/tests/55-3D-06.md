## Setup

The model is a 256-sided disc, its corners at radius 60 mm (120 mm, 4.724 in, across the corners), imported from the test fixture `Tests/CAMCoreTests/Fixtures/model-outline/disc-r60-256.stl` and centred on the board. An outside profile with the 1/4 in end mill cuts it out through the board on 4 tabs. No 3D toolpath carves it: the test is the outline alone.

## Steps

1. Level 0. In the app the outline drawn round the model follows its facets.
2. Level 1, then Level 3.
3. Measure the disc across opposite corners in at least four directions, and across opposite flats.

## Pass criteria

| Check | Target | Tolerance | Measured | ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diameter across the corners, 4 directions | 120.00 mm (4.724 in) | +0.13 / −0 mm (never smaller, less bit deflection) | | |
| Facets visible as 256 even flats (look closely; each is 1.5 mm long) | Yes | — | | |
| Tabs intact | Yes | — | | |

## Failure and what to report

A disc smaller than 120 mm across the corners by more than the bit's deflection means the profile cut inside the model's outline (a gouge): report it as a safety finding, with the file and the measurements.
