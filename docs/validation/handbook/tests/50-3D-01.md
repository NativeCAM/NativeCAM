## Setup

The model is a relief 0.400 in (10.16 mm) deep inside an oval medallion 6.02 × 4.06 in, cut out on 4 tabs. It's the same as the app's **Demo Relief**.

## Steps

1. Level 0: three toolpaths, T1, then T7, then T1 again (two changes).
2. Level 1: watch the first roughing layer and the first rows of the finish. The finish's rows link with arcs, staying inside the oval.
3. Level 2 in foam: compare the shape with the picture.
4. Level 3. Re-zero Z on the material top at both changes (the ball nose on its tip). Time the job.

## Failure and what to report

A step over the whole finished surface after the tool change (Z re-zero error, or the ball nose zeroed somewhere other than its tip), gouges (photograph and note where), or a much longer run than estimated (note your look-ahead setting).
