title: Surfacing a board at the defaults
family: surfacing
validates: A surfacing program for a 12 × 12 in board at the default stepover and depth: the 3 s dwell before the first contact, the entry in free air past the board's edge, the load, and the edges
levels: 0, 1, 3
risk: Medium (a large bit)
time: 20 min
stock: A 12 × 12 × 3/4 in MDF board screwed or clamped down, fasteners outside the area or 1/8 in below the cut
bits: T8 1-1/4 in surfacing bit
commands:
- nativecam-cli surface --area 12x12 --board 0.75 -o surface-board-12x12.tap
---
## Steps

1. Level 0: lines 50% of the bit apart, 0.020 in deep, with the overshoot past each end. The board's thickness is checked (at least 1/2 in left).
2. Level 1 with the air cut, `surface-board-12x12 (air cut).tap`: every rapid at or above the clearance; the entry plunges in free air off the board's edge.
3. Level 3. Listen for load; check the 3 s dwell comes before the first contact; check the edges for lips.

## Pass criteria

| Check | Target | Tolerance | Measured | ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spindle dwells at least 3 s before the first contact | Yes | — | | |
| First contact from free air past the board's edge, not a plunge into it | Yes | — | | |
| Surface flat and even, no lips at the edges | Yes | — | | |
| Depth removed | 0.020 in | ±0.003 in | | |

## Failure and what to report

The program, the warnings, and photos of any lip or ridge.
