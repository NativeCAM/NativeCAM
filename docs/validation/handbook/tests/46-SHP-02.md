## Setup

Set each form bit's real profile in the tool library before you post. Remove the bearing, and touch off on the bottom of the cutting edge. The warning about the roundover plunging into uncut material on one lap is expected for this project: watch that lap closely in the air cut, or clear the waste with an end mill first.

## Steps

1. Level 0. Check the setup sheet's tools: their diameters and profiles must match yours.
2. Level 1: watch the roundover's lead ramp down in the waste, the laps step in, and the links between them stay in the waste. Watch the cove's ramp entry.
3. Level 3 with the spindle at the setup sheet's RPM. Listen for chatter.

## Failure and what to report

Steps in the profile, burning (note the RPM and feed), the bit plunging into uncut wood. Photos of the edge from the side.
