## Setup

The part is a 120 × 80 mm rectangle centred at X 100, Y 75 mm, with a 50.8 mm hole. The cut-out's four tabs are placed by hand: on the bottom edge 30 mm left of centre, on the top edge 30 mm right of centre, on the left edge at its middle, and on the right edge 10 mm above its middle. The hole has two evenly spaced triangular tabs. The cut-out starts at the middle of the top (back) edge.

## Steps

1. Level 0 and the setup sheet: it lists the tabs and the start point.
2. Level 1. Watch Z ramp smoothly up and over each triangular tab on the last passes, with no stall (under `G64` on Mach4), and the cut-out begin at the top edge's middle.
3. Level 3. Before freeing the part, check every tab held through the last pass. Measure each tab's thickness at its centre, and where each sits.

Note your controller's corner-blending setting (Mach4: CV Dist Tolerance) with the result: it decides how much the tab ramps' corners are rounded.

## Failure and what to report

A tab missing, too thin, or in the wrong place; a stall on the ramps; a step at the seam. Photos and measurements.
