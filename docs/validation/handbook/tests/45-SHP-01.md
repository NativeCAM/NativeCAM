## Setup

A 6 × 3 in panel at (5, 5) in gets a 0.100 in chamfer round its outside top edge (cut in two passes). Three flutes run along X at Y 1.25, 1.75 and 2.25 in, 0.080 in deep below a 0.020 in start depth, ramping in over their first 0.5 in. A diamond (a four-sided polygon 1 in across its corners) at (9, 2) in is raised to a ridge, 0.200 in deep.

The panel isn't cut out: the chamfer runs round its outline as a V-groove's outer face.

## Steps

1. Level 0. Three bits, two tool changes.
2. Level 1, then Level 3.
3. Measure the chamfer's width on the top face, the flutes' depth at their deep end and the length of their ramp, and the prism's ridge.

## Pass criteria

| Check | Target | Tolerance | Measured | ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chamfer width on the top face | 0.100 in (2.54 mm) | ±0.010 in | | |
| Chamfer at 45°, corners sharp and mitred | Yes | — | | |
| Flute depth below the top, deep end | 0.100 in (2.54 mm) | ±0.005 in | | |
| Flute ramp length | about 0.5 in | — | | |
| Prism: four faces meeting in one ridge point at the centre | Yes | — | | |
| Prism depth at the outline | 0.200 in (5.08 mm) | ±0.010 in | | |

## Failure and what to report

A chamfer of the wrong width (Z zero or bit angle), a flute at the wrong depth, or a prism's faces not meeting. Photos.
