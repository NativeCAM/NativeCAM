## Setup

Clamp the MDF at its corners, outside the area between X 1 and 9 in and Y 0.75 and 6.5 in (the setup sheet's extents). The outside square stays on 4 tabs; the hole's slug comes free, so stop and remove it if it lifts.

## Steps

1. Level 0 and the air cut (Level 1), as in VH-BAS-01 and VH-BAS-02. Watch the hole's and the square's lead-ins arc onto the wall from the waste side, and the ramp entries descend along the lead, not plunge.
2. Level 2 in foam (optional): check positions and the tabs.
3. Level 3: cut it. Measure before and after freeing the square from its tabs.

## Failure and what to report

A part oversize or undersize by about the bit's diameter means the cut was on the wrong side; by a few thousandths, deflection (report it, with your feed). A witness mark on the part's wall at the lead-in, tabs missing or too thin, or a groove off-centre: photograph it and report the values.
