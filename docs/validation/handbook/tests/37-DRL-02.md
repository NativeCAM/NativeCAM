## Steps

1. Level 0. The helices are `G03` arcs with a Z word on each, a quarter turn per line.
2. Level 1. Watch the helices run smoothly, without pausing between quarter-turns (under `G64` on Mach4). Time the whole air cut against the estimate.
3. Level 3. Measure every hole in X and Y. Try a 3/8 in dowel, a 1/2 in bolt and a 35 mm hinge cup in their holes.

## Failure and what to report

Holes oval (measure X and Y separately), small by the bit's deflection, or with a spiral mark on the wall. Note the wall finish, and report each measurement.
