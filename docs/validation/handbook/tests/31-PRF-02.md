## Steps

1. Level 0. In the file, find the finish laps: a lap offset 0.020 in (0.508 mm) inside the roughing for the part, and the feed change at the finish's lead-in.
2. Level 1. Watch the roughing laps, then the finish lap with its lead-in, then the spring pass (a second lap at the same size) on the outside part. Watch for a pause or stall at the feed change (Mach4's constant-velocity blending through a change of F is untested).
3. Level 3. Measure the part and the hole on all four sides.
4. If you can, cut a second part with the finishing pass turned off (in the app) and compare the edge: fuzz, and the witness mark where the cut started.

## Failure and what to report

A part 1 mm (0.040 in) too small or a hole 1 mm too big would mean the finish was on the wrong side. Report the measurements, photos of the edges, and whether the spring pass changed the size.
