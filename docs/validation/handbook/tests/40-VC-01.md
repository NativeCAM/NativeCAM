## Setup

"NC" in 1.5 in capitals at the upper left; a 2.5 × 1.5 in rectangle at the upper right, carved flat at 0.125 in with the 1/8 in end mill clearing the flat; a 6 × 0.75 in strip at the bottom, carved flat at 0.0625 in by the V-bit alone. Zero Z with care: every line's width depends on it.

## Steps

1. Level 0. Note the toolpath order in the file and the tool changes (the info line suggests a run order with one fewer change; leave it as it is).
2. Level 1, then Level 2 in foam to see the letters' shapes.
3. Level 3. Measure the rectangle's floor and its walls, and look at the floor's corners and the letters' corners.

## Failure and what to report

Lines all too wide or too narrow by the same amount mean a Z zero error (or a bit angle that isn't 60°). A floor with a step at the edge of the end mill's area means a re-zero error after the change. A nub left in a corner of the rectangle's floor means the V-bit didn't clean the corner: note which corner, and its size. Photos under raking light.
