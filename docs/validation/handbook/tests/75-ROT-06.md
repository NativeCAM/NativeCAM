## Setup

The app's **Demo Pawn**. Set your **turn speed** (from VH-ROT-02, or Mach4's A motor velocity) and centre height under **Rotary Axis**, and post again: the warnings about the assumed 90° a second then change to your axis's real limit. Zero on the axis.

## Steps

1. Level 0: two toolpaths, both "all round". Note the turn-speed and chip-load warnings near the axis: they're expected until the turn speed is set.
2. Level 1: the air cut, Z raised 1 in. Start with the feed override turned down.
3. Level 3. Part the pawn off at its stubs by hand.

## Failure and what to report

An oval or faceted pawn (`G93` or turn speed), burn marks near the axis (note the RPM), or a much longer run than estimated. The file, the controller, your turn speed, and photos.
