## Steps

1. Zero X, Y and Z somewhere you can find again (a pencil cross on the spoilboard), and note the G54 values your controller shows. On a rotary machine, turn A to a known angle (say 45°) and note the A position.
2. Turn the controller and the machine off at the wall. Wait 30 s. Turn them on, and home the machine.
3. Without re-zeroing, jog to X0 Y0 and lower Z to just above the cross. Read the G54 values and the A position again.

## Pass criteria

| Check | Target | Measured | ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|
| G54 X, Y, Z unchanged after the power cut and re-home (note each) | Unchanged | | |
| Tool lands on the cross after re-homing | Yes | | |
| A position after the power cut (note it) | — | | |

A controller that loses G54 needs the XY zero set again before any restart: the restart header says to check it. Write what yours does on the sign-off sheet.

## Failure and what to report

Your controller and version, and what changed.
