## Steps

1. Make the air cut and the normal file (VH-BAS-01). Compare them in a text editor or a diff tool: every line should match except the Z words (each raised by exactly 1.0000 in), the safe and park heights, and the air-cut comment at the top.
2. Zero X, Y and Z on the coupon's MDF as for the real cut (lower left, top).
3. Run the air cut with the spindle running. Watch the lowest point of each pass: the bit's tip should stop about 1 in above the MDF. Hold a rule against the bit at the deepest pass of the square if you like: 1.000 − 0.770 = 0.230 in above the top.
4. Watch the order: the groove, the hole (ramping down on its lead-in), then the square, lifting over 4 tabs on its last pass.

## Pass criteria

| Check | Target | Measured | ✓ |

## Failure and what to report

Both files (normal and air cut), and where in the program the tip came closer than expected.
