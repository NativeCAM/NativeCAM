## Steps

1. Level 0. Read the restart's header: the toolpath and pass (`Pocket with island, pass 2 of 11`), the tool to load, re-home and check XY after a power cut or E-stop, re-zero Z, jog Z clear before Cycle Start, and the stop over the restart point. Read the warning about the spin-up dwell (0 s on the default machine; the stop over the restart point dwells 3 s anyway).
2. Level 1. The restart is saved as `pkt-island-restart-air (air cut) (restart at toolpath 1 pass 2).tap`: the CLI adds where it restarts to the name. Jog Z clear, then run it and watch, in order:
   - the tool change (Avid: the M6 macro), and whether **Z rises before X and Y move**;
   - the move to the restart point at safe Z;
   - the stop with the spindle off, and any message (`MSG`) your controller shows;
   - the spindle restart and its dwell;
   - the plunge at the plunge feed, then the rest of the job as in the full file.

## Failure and what to report

A rapid down to the work, XY moving before Z rises, or a plunge with the spindle off: safety findings, report them at once with both files.
