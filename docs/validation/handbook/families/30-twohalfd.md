# Profiles, pockets and drilling

The 2.5D toolpaths most jobs are made of. Each coupon fits on a board 10 × 8 in (250 × 200 mm) or smaller, and most can be cut on one 12 × 12 in sheet of MDF if you place them yourself.

Measure parts with calipers while they're still in place if you can, then again after removing them from their tabs. Inside sizes (holes, pockets, mortises) are the hardest for a machine: deflection makes holes small and parts large by a few thousandths. Report the measured value either way.
