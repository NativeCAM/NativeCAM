# 3D roughing and finishing

3D jobs run long programs of many short moves. They test how well your controller's look-ahead keeps the feed up, and they're where a wrong Z zero after a tool change shows most. Re-zero Z with the touch plate after every change, and air-cut each program first: they're long, so watch at least the first layer of roughing and the first rows of the finish.

Ball-nose bits are zeroed on the tip, as the starter library defines them.
