# V-carving and inlays

V-carving depends on the V-bit's angle and point being exactly what the tool library says, and on Z zero being exactly on the surface: a Z zero 0.010 in low widens every line by 0.012 in with a 60° bit. Zero carefully with the touch plate, and use a sharp-pointed bit whose angle you know.

For inlays, check the bit's angle with the plug test before gluing anything: an angle that differs between the two halves stops the plug seating.
