# What you need

Every test fits on small stock: nothing needs a board bigger than 12 × 12 in (300 × 300 mm) except the tiling test, whose point is a board longer than one tile (8 × 20 in, but you choose the tile length), and the optional waterline test (300 × 150 × 40 mm). You don't need everything: buy for the families you'll run.

## Materials

| Material | Size | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| MDF, 3/4 in (18–19 mm) | 4 or 5 pieces 12 × 12 in, 2 pieces 8 × 6 in | Most 2.5D and 3D tests |
| Rigid foam (XPS insulation board), 1 in or thicker | 2–3 pieces 12 × 12 in | Level 2 shallow cuts, gentler first runs |
| Hardwood (maple, cherry or similar), 3/4 in | 1 piece 8 × 5 in, 1 piece 6 × 5 × 1 in, 1 piece 6 × 4 in | V-carve, inlay pocket, adaptive pocket |
| Contrasting hardwood, 1/2–3/4 in | 1 piece 6 × 5 in | Inlay plug |
| Plywood, 3/4 in (18 mm) | 2 pieces 12 × 12 in, 1 piece 8 × 20 in | Nesting, tiling, finishing pass |
| Softwood or poplar, square and round | 2 in square × 12 in; 2 in dowel × 10 in; 1.75 in square × 6 in; 2 in square × 10 in | Rotary tests (only with a rotary axis) |
| Scrap of any kind | 12 × 12 in | Air-cut target, calibration ladder |

## Bits

All are 2-flute carbide unless noted. The kit's projects use the starter library's numbers; any bit of the same size and shape will do, as long as you set its real dimensions in the tool library.

| T | Bit | Used for | Needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 1/4 in (6.35 mm) upcut end mill, 3/4 in flutes | Almost every test | Yes |
| T2 | 1/8 in (3.175 mm) upcut end mill, 1/2 in flutes | Rest machining, inlay clearing, holes | Yes |
| T5 | 60° V-bit, 1/2 in, sharp point | V-carve, inlays, prism | For V-carve |
| T6 | 90° V-bit, 1/2 in | Chamfer | For chamfers |
| T7 | 1/4 in ball nose | 3D finishing, flutes | For 3D |
| T11 | 1/8 in ball nose | 3D rest finishing | For 3D rest |
| T10 | 1/2 in upcut end mill | Two-tool pocket | Optional |
| T8 | 1-1/4 in surfacing bit (the starter library's) | Spoilboard surfacing | For surfacing |
| — | Form bits: 1-1/2 in roundover, 1-1/4 in cove | Edge profile | Optional |

## Measuring tools

- [ ] Digital calipers, 6 in (150 mm), reading to 0.001 in / 0.01 mm
- [ ] Depth gauge (the calipers' depth rod will do), or a dial depth gauge
- [ ] Steel rule, 12 in (300 mm), and a combination square
- [ ] Radius gauges, 1/16–1/4 in (1.5–6 mm), for inside corners
- [ ] A stopwatch, or your phone's (calibration and run times)
- [ ] Feeler gauges (inlay glue gap, step heights)
- [ ] A fine marker or pencil, and a camera for photos

## Machine accessories

- [ ] Touch plate (or your usual way of zeroing Z)
- [ ] Clamps or screws, and a few 1/4 in (6 mm) dowels for the tiling test
- [ ] For rotary tests: the rotary axis or indexer, its chuck and tailstock
